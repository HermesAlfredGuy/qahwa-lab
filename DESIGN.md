# QAHWA LAB — DESIGN.md

**Concept** — The site is a roast session. One Khawlani bean, lit like a product macro, is roasted live by scroll from green to 212 °C, and the page below reads like the lab's log: numbers first, adjectives almost never.

**Palette** — `#100c0a` ground (roast black, warm) · `#191310` / `#221a15` panels · `#f4ebdf` ink · `#a2917f` / `#9b8875` secondary ink · `#c8642a` burnt amber (accent, <5% of pixels) · `#9aa863` raw-bean green (lab data only) · `#ece2d2` paper (the one light section, the contour map).

**Type** — Display: Gloock 400 (warm high-contrast serif, used huge and tight). Text: Figtree Variable. Data: DM Mono. Arabic: Aref Ruqaa (خولاني) + Reem Kufi (مختبر القهوة).

**Motion character** — Heavy, heat-driven, never bouncy: long expo-outs, scroll-scrubbed state rather than triggers, inertia that arrives like a drum settling. The bean turns slowly and keeps turning.

**Signature moment** — The pinned hero: scrolling roasts the bean (colour, roughness, clearcoat, relief, light and reflection all interpolate), while the roast log counts 200 °C → 92 °C turning point → 196 °C first crack → 212 °C drop, and the title steps back. It ends with the bean dropping out of frame.

**Second moment** — The mountains and the roast drawn as the same line language: hand-built contour lines of the Jazan escarpment draw on in the paper section, then the roast curve draws itself in the dark section.

**Avoid** — (1) dark-gradient AI look with purple/blue bloom, (2) jute/burlap stock photography and steam god-rays, (3) uniform card grids with fake prices. No invented prices, stock counts or certifications anywhere.

**Differs from the last two projects** — Afterlight was a full 3D world (game), Chroma was a motion film; this is typography-first editorial with exactly one 3D object, and the 3D earns its place because the product literally changes state with heat.

**Phone rule** — Built at 390 × 844 first: one full-bleed bean, one headline that never wraps, HUD in the bottom safe area, swipeable lot rail.
